**Industry → Reactions management.** Plans composite and molecular-forged reactions from CCP's formulas: what to run in each slot, what to buy, and what you earn per week. Follow the four numbered panels.

## 1 · Setup
Your reaction slots and how you buy and sell:
- **Reaction slots** and **Reactions skill** come from your characters; **Cycle** is how long one run of jobs lasts.
- **System**, **Refinery** and **Facility tax**: where you react. Rigs count the same way as in Build planning.
- **Inputs at**, **Fuel blocks at**, **Outputs at**: the [pricings](Pricings) used. **Sold by**: contract, immediate sale or sell order.
- **Delivery system** and **To the reactor with**: how inputs get to the refinery and products back (jump freighter or freight). Import and export hauling are counted separately.

## 2 · Pick reactions
Reactions by kind (folding groups), with what one slot of each earns per cycle. Sort by any column. Set **how many instances** of each to run.
- The chains are balanced for you: intermediates are planned, not picked (one slot feeding intermediates usually feeds two composite jobs).

## 3 · Shopping
Fuel blocks, moon materials, other inputs and formulas to buy, **after what you already hold** (formulas: every one your pilots and corporation own, in a hangar, a stack or a running job). Choose whether your stock counts from every hangar or only the build system. The same list shows on [To-Do](To-Do) as **Buy for reactions**, with ticks.

## 4 · React
The jobs to start each cycle and what you sell at its end. Every job of a reaction runs the same number of runs (shown as "4 × 123").
- **Build-up phase:** for the first cycles, when intermediates aren't made yet.
- **Full chain:** once everything runs.
- Click counters keep track of the jobs you've started.

## To the Forecast
**Add to the Forecast** puts the plan's profit per week on the [Overview](Overview). It follows your changes from then on.
