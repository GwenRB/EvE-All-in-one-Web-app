**Market → Contract management.** Orders you take for clients: what they want, what you've sent, what's still missing. Listed oldest first.

## New contract
1. **Client:** their name (the box remembers past clients).
2. Paste the goods they want: an inventory copy, a multibuy list, an EFT fit, `Name x100`, `100 Name`…
3. **Create contract.**

**Open in Janice** copies what's in the box and opens Janice in a new tab, ready to paste.

## Recording what you send
Open an order. Its title is the client's name (click to copy it). Each time you send goods, paste what you sent in **Record a send**. **Still missing** updates.

## In-game deliveries
The tool finds item exchange contracts your pilots made to the client in game (for themselves, or for the corporation), so you don't have to paste them. Needs the **Contracts** permission (and **Corporation contracts** for corporation ones); read at each sync.
- **Record as sent** (or **Record all**) adds a contract's items as a send.
- Contracts that failed (rejected, expired…) aren't offered. A recorded one that fails later is flagged "not delivered".
- The order list shows "n in game to record".
- The box always says what it looked for and when contracts were last read.

### The client plays under another name?
Click **Not finding them?**: it lists your item exchanges to other characters since the order. **This is &lt;client&gt;** links one of them to the order (✕ unlinks).

Open orders also show on [To-Do](To-Do) under **Ongoing contracts**.
