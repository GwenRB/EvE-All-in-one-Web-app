**Item database**, in the **Other** menu. Every item in the game, from CCP's own game data (the same data every page uses, read once per game build and kept in your browser). No permission needed.

## Find an item
1. Type part of its name in **Find an item** (several words must all be in the name).
2. Or pick a **category**, then a **group**.
3. Tick **Include items not in the game** to also see CCP's unpublished entries.
4. Use [Filters](The-basics#filters) for the rest: volume, packaged volume, mass, capacity, CCP base price, tech level, meta group, race, *Made by* (manufacturing, reaction, invention), *Used to make things*, *Can be reprocessed*, *You own*…

Results are grouped by category and group (fold them like any grouped table) and sorted by the column you click. Each category shows its first 100 items; its **Show more** line shows more of that category.

Items sold on the market have a small chart button next to their name: it opens the item on the [Trading board](Trading-board) (every order for it, the hubs side by side, its price history). An opened item has **View on the Trading board** in its title bar.

## Open an item
Click its name. The panel below shows:
- **What it is:** type ID, group, category, market group, tech level, meta group, race, volume, packaged volume, mass, capacity, portion size and CCP's base price (a reference value, not a market price).
- **Prices** in the pricing you pick there, Jita sell and buy, ESI's average price and ESI's adjusted price (what job costs use). "No price" when nobody sells it.
- **You own:** how many sit in the hangars read from ESI, and where.
- **Skills to use it**, **Reprocesses into** (before yield) and the **Bonuses** of a ship.
- **Made by:** the blueprint or formula, with what one run needs and makes, its time and its skills. A blueprint also shows **What this blueprint does** (manufacturing, invention, copying, research).
- **Used in:** every blueprint and planet factory that takes it as a material.
- **Attributes:** the ones the game shows, with their names and units (read from ESI the first time). Tick **Show every attribute** to see the hidden ones too.

Every item name in the panel is a link: click it to open that item. **Copy the name** puts it on your clipboard; **Close** hides the panel.
