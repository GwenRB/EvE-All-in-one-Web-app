**Industry → PI Management.** A P4 planner for **factory-only planets**: you buy the P1 and P2, your planets make the P3 and the P4, you sell the P4. Recipes come from CCP's planet schematics.

## P4 planner · Settings
- **Planets you have**, and how many **P4 plants** you put on each kind of planet ("P1 + 2×P3" and "3×P3" planets).
- **Customs office tax:** type it, or leave it **empty** to use the tax your pilots really pay at your corporation's customs offices in the planets' system (needs a Director with the **customs offices** permission). Several rates: the highest is used. None known: 0%. Imports pay half.
- **Inputs at … bought by**, **P4 at … sold by**: the [pricings](Pricings), and how you buy and sell. **Broker fee**, **Sales tax**, **Market history**.
- **Delivery system**, **Planets' system**, **Between them with**: how things move (jump freighter or freight). Optional Jita legs for importing inputs and exporting the P4.

## Pick production
Which P4 pays best right now per planet, grouped "P1 + two P3" and "three P3". Set how many planets you give each. A warning shows when your output would be a big share of Jita's volume.

## Shopping
Every P1 and P2 the planets need for the **days of stock** you choose, minus what your hangars hold:
- **Needed**, **In stock**, **Remaining** (how long your stock lasts at the planets' use, red under a day), **To buy**, **Cost**.
- The same list shows on [To-Do](To-Do) as **Buy for PI**, with ticks.

## Output and Planet batches
- **Output:** what the planets make over the days of stock and what it's worth.
- **Planet batches:** what to drop at each planet. Load the hauler once, then fill planets one by one.

## To the Forecast
**Add to the Forecast** puts the profit per week on the [Overview](Overview).

Your running colonies themselves are on the [Planets](Planets) page.
