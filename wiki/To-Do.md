Everything waiting on you, in one list. The left navigation lists the panels that have something in them (one line each), and the To-Do tab shows a red count.

| List | What's in it | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Omega to renew** | Accounts whose Omega ends within 7 days | [Omega](Omega) |
| **Structures to refuel** | Corporation structures running out of fuel within your warning (1–30 days, default 7) | [Corporation](Corporation) |
| **Skill queues to fill** | Pilots whose queue is empty or paused | [Skills](Skills) |
| **Empty job slots** | One row per pilot: empty manufacturing, science and reaction slots, and finished jobs still to deliver (they hold their slot until then) | ESI jobs |
| **Ongoing contracts** | Client orders still missing goods, and your open contracts in game | [Contract management](Contract-management) |
| **Trips for a freight service** | Hauling none of your ships can do | [Supply chain](Supply-chain) |
| **Buy for reactions** | What the reactions plan still needs after your stock | [Reactions management](Reactions-management) |
| **Buy for PI** | P1 and P2 for PI's days of stock, after your stock | [PI Management](PI-Management) |
| **Buy for builds** | Materials for Planned and In progress builds | [Orders management](Orders-management-and-Jobs) |

## Ticking off
Shopping lists have a tick box per line: tick each one as you buy it.

## Choosing what counts
- **Skill queues:** untick a pilot on the [Skills](Skills) page to stop seeing them. Pilots idle for **more than 7 days** only show (and count on the tab) when you tick the box in this panel.
- **Empty job slots:** the **Show** chips pick the kinds of slot (manufacturing, science, reactions) and the pilots listed; what you leave out doesn't count in the tab's number either. ✕ hides one kind of slot for one pilot (for example a pilot who never does science); **Show them again** brings them back. Empty slots only count in the tab's number when you tick **Count in the To-Do tab's number** (off by default).
- **Freight trips:** once you've contracted one out, mark it **Sent by freight**.
