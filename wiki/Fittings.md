**Pilots → Fittings.** See which pilots can fly a fit, and when the others will.

## Add a fitting
- **Paste:** in game, open the fitting window and use **Copy to clipboard**, then paste here (EFT format) and **Add fitting**.
- **In-game fittings:** imported from ESI (needs the **Fittings saved in game** permission).

## For each fit
- **Can fly it:** every skill the hull, modules, rigs, drones and fighters need. Levels the skill queue has finished since the last sync count.
- **Time to fly it**, for everyone else, soonest first:
  - when every missing level is already in their queue: the date the last one finishes ("ready …");
  - otherwise: the time to train just what's missing, prerequisites included, from their attributes and implants (~ when attributes weren't read yet).
- **Required skills and checked items.**

Time to fly it and Required skills are folded by default.

## Doctrines
Tick **Doctrine** on a fit to have it checked on the [Shortages](Shortages) page.

Fittings added long ago (before doctrines existed) only kept hull, modules and drones: add them again for a full check.
