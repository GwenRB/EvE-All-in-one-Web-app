## Your first day
1. Start the launcher and open http://localhost:8642/ ([Getting started](Getting-started)).
2. Create your EVE application and link every character ([Getting started](Getting-started#3-create-your-eve-application-one-time), [ESI permissions](ESI-permissions)).
3. **Map → Citadels:** refresh from ESI, then add the structures you build, react and refine in, with their rigs ([Map and jump planner](Map-and-jump-planner#citadels)).
4. **Settings → Pricings and HUBs:** add your citadel markets or buyback rates ([Pricings](Pricings)). **Market taxes:** check them.
5. **Pilots → Omega:** enter each account's end date ([Omega](Omega)).
6. **Settings → Export backup** ([Settings and backups](Settings-and-backups#backups)).

## Build a ship or module and haul it all
1. **Industry → Build planning:** search the blueprint, set the quantity, **Plan the build** ([Build planning](Build-planning)).
2. Check **Structures and settings** at the bottom: the right structures, rigs and hauling.
3. Look at the build tree: switch components you'd rather buy to **buy**. For T2, compare decryptors in **Invention**. Check **Build or buy** to see if public contracts are cheaper.
4. Optional: turn on **Ore instead of minerals** if you reprocess.
5. **Send to Orders management.**
6. Buy what's listed under **Buy for builds** on [To-Do](To-Do), ticking as you go.
7. **Map → Supply chain:** pick **Plan for** and **Plan ahead**, and fly the trips it gives ([Supply chain](Supply-chain)). Moves are marked done as your assets show the goods arriving.
8. Start the jobs and set the order **In progress** in [Orders management](Orders-management-and-Jobs). The [Forecast](Overview) now counts it on the day it should finish.
9. When it's delivered, set it **Done**.

## Run a reaction chain
1. **Industry → Reactions management → 1 · Setup:** your refinery, system, pricings and hauling ([Reactions management](Reactions-management)).
2. **2 · Pick reactions:** sort by profit, set the instances of each.
3. **3 · Shopping:** buy what's listed (also on To-Do as **Buy for reactions**).
4. **4 · React:** start with **Build-up phase**, then switch to **Full chain**. Use the counters as you start jobs.
5. **Add to the Forecast.**
6. If the reactor isn't in your delivery system, the [Supply chain](Supply-chain) plans the cycles' hauling.

## Set up P4 production
1. **Industry → PI Management → Settings:** your planets, plants per planet, systems and pricings. Leave the customs tax empty to use your corporation's real rates ([PI Management](PI-Management)).
2. **Pick production:** choose the P4 and give it planets.
3. Choose your **days of stock**, then buy the **Shopping** list (also on To-Do as **Buy for PI**). Watch **Remaining**.
4. Load the hauler once and drop off at each planet using **Planet batches**.
5. **Planets:** tick **I harvest daily at** with your usual time ([Planets](Planets)).
6. **Add to the Forecast.** The [Supply chain](Supply-chain) plans the P4 pickups.

## Fill a client order
1. **Market → Contract management:** client name, paste what they want, **Create contract** ([Contract management](Contract-management)).
2. Build or buy the goods (see above).
3. Make the item exchange contract to the client in game.
4. After the next sync, open the order: the contract is found under **In-game deliveries**. **Record as sent**.
5. When **Still missing** is empty, the order is complete.

## Your daily check
1. **To-Do:** everything waiting, with the red count on its tab ([To-Do](To-Do)).
2. **Intel:** anything under **Needs attention**, then **Mark all as seen** ([Intel](Intel)).
3. **Planets:** colonies stopping today.
4. **Overview:** calendar and upcoming events.

## See what you really earned this month
1. **Market → Profit and loss**, period **This month** ([Profit and loss](Profit-and-loss)).
2. Look at the **Not assigned** tile. Sort those once in **Items**, **Who you dealt with** or **Kinds of entry**; every later entry of the same kind follows.
3. **By month** compares activities over time.
4. The [Forecast](Overview) shows your actual profit next to the planned one.
