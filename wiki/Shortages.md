**Market → Shortages.** **Doctrine shortages:** your doctrine fittings checked against a citadel's market.

1. On the [Fittings](Fittings) page, tick **Doctrine** on the fits that are doctrines.
2. Here, pick the citadel (through a citadel [pricing](Pricings), or any known citadel).
3. Set how many fits to keep on sale, per doctrine.

You get:
- **Nobody sells these:** items no one sells there.
- **Low** items: fewer than your fits to keep.
- **Full fits** on sale.
- **Items to seed**, with a multibuy copy ready to paste in game.
