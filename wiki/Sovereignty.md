**Sovereignty** (the last tab). Who holds null-sec, from CCP's own public data (ESI), like DOTLAN's alliance page.

## Alliances
Every alliance holding sovereignty, ranked by the systems it holds (with, in brackets, its share of all the systems sovereignty can be held in), with its members (its corporations' members added up) and how many corporations it has. Type in **Find an alliance** to filter by name or ticker. **Map** next to an alliance (or a coalition) opens the [Map](Map-and-jump-planner) framed on its systems, each with a green ring and the rest dimmed; **Hide** in the map's legend clears it.

The first time, the page reads every corporation of those alliances (a few minutes; the status says how far it is). It's then kept a day in your browser. **Refresh from ESI** reads the map and the alliances again.

## Coalitions
Your own groups of alliances.
1. Type a name and click **Add coalition**.
2. In the Alliances table, pick the coalition in the **Coalition** list next to each alliance (— takes it out).

Each coalition lists its alliances by members (most first) and adds up the systems (with their share, %), members and corporations of its alliances. On the [Map](Map-and-jump-planner), coalitions can be **Allies**, and the one your pilots' alliance is in is by default. **Remove** deletes it (its alliances go back to no coalition). Coalitions are kept in backups.

On a wide screen the next two panels sit just right of the page (like the navigation on the left of other pages) and stay in view while you scroll; on a narrow one, under it. Long names end in …: hover for the full name.

## Top sov changes (7 days)
The alliances that gained or lost the most systems: **Gained**, **Lost** and the **Change**.

## Alliance movements (7 days)
Corporations that changed alliance (biggest first), from and to which alliance. Only corporations of alliances holding sovereignty (before or now) are followed.

Both compare today with what the tool saw 7 days ago. It keeps one snapshot a day while you open this page, in this browser only (not in backups), so the first week counts from the oldest one it has ("Since …"), and the first day shows nothing yet.
