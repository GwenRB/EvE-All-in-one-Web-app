## Pilots and backup
- **Add pilot:** a pilot by hand, without ESI (see [Characters](Characters#pilots-without-esi)). Linked pilots come from **Link EVE characters** at the top of the page.
- **Export backup** / **Import backup:** see below.

## Backups
Everything is stored in your browser. A backup is a file with everything the tool keeps: pilots, jobs, planets, wallets and their history, hangars, contracts, forecast, fittings, the ship scan, the map, citadels, settings and your Client ID.

**When the browser's storage is full** the tool says "Your browser blocked saving" once: changes from then on may not be kept. Export a backup right away.

**Not in backups:** login tokens (log in again after a move), moon mining and in-game notifications (kept 90 days in the browser only).

To move to another browser or computer:
1. **Settings → Export backup** on the old one.
2. Start the tool on the new one, **Settings → Import backup**.
3. Log each pilot in again.

Importing **joins** the backup's Profit and loss history with what's already there; it never replaces it. Export a backup now and then: clearing your browser's site data deletes everything.

## Pages
Untick a page to hide its tab. Settings always stays.

## Pricings and HUBs
Your pricings and the trade hubs every market menu offers. See [Pricings](Pricings) (its last section is the trade hubs).

## Ore reprocessing
The **best possible yield %**, used to value ore and ice on [Assets](Assets) and mined ore on [Corporation](Corporation) (about 90.6%: Tatara with tech II rigs in null-sec, all skills at V, a 4% implant). [Build planning](Build-planning#ore-instead-of-minerals-optional-off-by-default) uses its own reprocessing places instead.

## Market taxes
- **Sales tax:** from your best pilot's Accounting, the base rate (7.5%), or your own value. It applies to every sale, instant sales to buy orders included.
- **Broker fee** in NPC stations (from Broker Relations and standings; standings need the **Standings** permission) and **in citadels** (the owner sets it; type it).

## Jump drive economizers
Build planning uses the best economizer you own (in a hangar, or fitted on or carried in a ship); when there's none, it uses the one you pick here.

## EvE News channels
What the [Overview's](Overview#eve-news) news panel shows:
- Untick a channel to hide it, type over a name to rename it, move channels up or down (an article two channels carry shows under the higher one).
- The launcher's news can't be removed, only unticked, and its categories (News, Patch notes, Dev blogs…) can be hidden one by one.
- **Add channel:** any RSS, Atom or JSON feed by its link (the tool reads it first to check it).
- **Reddit r/Eve: top today** is on by default: the day's 5 most upvoted posts. Reddit only allows about one read a minute, so if it shows an error, wait a minute and press **Refresh**.

## Time zones
The Overview's clocks, after EVE time: rename, move, remove, or add any city by its time zone (*Tokyo*, or *Asia/Tokyo*).

## Notes
The **Notes** tab: your own notes, saved as you type, kept in backups. **Download as text** saves them to a file.
