**Pilots → Skills.**

## Skill check
Search a blueprint to see **who can build or invent it**. Blueprints you check are saved in a list; ✕ removes one. The first check of a blueprint needs internet (CCP's game data); after that it works offline.

## Skill queues
What each pilot is training, levels left, when the queue ends and time left; the one running out first on top. Needs the **Skill queues** permission.
- Two groups: **Training now**, and **Not training** (empty, paused or not read). Only the first shows until you tick **Show pilots not training**.
- Queues age with the clock: a level whose finish date has passed counts as done.
- An empty or paused queue shows on [To-Do](To-Do). Untick a pilot here to stop that.

## Key skills
Each line is a skill at the level you count as a requirement; change a target with its dropdown. A green cell means that pilot meets it.

## Skill matrix
Every skill of every pilot, grouped (the groups fold, and appear in the left navigation). Besides the search, [Filters](The-basics#filters) work on the skill, its group, the highest and lowest level, how many pilots have it, who has it at V, or one pilot's level.
