**Stock → Assets.** What sits in your personal hangars and corporation hangar divisions, valued in the [pricing](Pricings) you choose (**Value at**).

Needs the **Personal hangars** permission (and **Corporation hangars** with a Director for corporation ones).

## Using it
- **Break down by** item category, item group, location, owner or hangar division; filter by category and group.
- **One row per item**, or **one row per item and hangar**.
- Items in containers in a hangar are counted. Ore and ice are valued at the minerals they give (at the yield in Settings → Ore reprocessing).
- Items with no price show empty and are listed, never counted as 0.
- **Where the value is** and **Hangars** sum it up, largest first.

## Find an item
The search box finds an item, group or category (for example *Tritanium*, *Mineral* or *Ship*) **everywhere**, including what isn't counted as stock:
- items in your **open contracts** (ESI takes them out of your assets while a contract is up), shown "in a contract" with its title;
- items **outside the hangars**: ship cargo and fittings, Deliveries, asset safety, containers in containers…, shown with where they are and marked "not counted".

These are shown so you can find them, but never count as stock on this or any other page.

**Filters** under the search box test each stack (one item in one hangar, container, ship or contract) on its item, group, category, quantity, unit price, value, owner, owner type, location, hangar or place, status (counted, not counted, in a contract) or blueprint copy. Like the search, they look in every hangar: add *Status is Counted* to keep only what counts. See [Filters](The-basics#filters).
