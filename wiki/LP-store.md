**Market → LP store.**

## Your loyalty points
LP each pilot holds per corporation, most first (needs the **Loyalty points** permission). Click a corporation to open its store.

## LP store
Any NPC corporation's store (the one where you have the most LP by default), every offer in **one list**, best **ISK per LP** first.
1. Pick **Sell items at**: the [pricing](Pricings) you'd sell the reward in.
2. Pick **Buy other items at**: the pricing for the extra items some offers ask for.

Per offer:
- **Sells for:** the item's price × quantity, after your sales tax.
- **Costs:** the offer's ISK plus the other items it asks for.
- **Profit**, **ISK per LP** = (sells for − costs) ÷ LP.
- **You can buy:** how many times your pilots' LP with that corporation pays for it.
- **Sold per day** in that pricing's region: red when under one offer's worth a day (slow to sell).

An item with no price leaves its offer's profit **unknown** (never 0). Blueprint copies usually have no price. Broker fees and Analysis Kredits aren't counted.

A store with more than 300 offers shows the best 300 first: **Show more** adds the next ones.

## CONCORD LP
CONCORD LP can be converted in game (docked at an NPC station) into the LP of many other corporations: **10 CONCORD LP give 8 LP** of an empire faction's corporation (Amarr, Caldari, Gallente, Minmatar, Ammatar, Khanid), **or 4 LP** of another faction's (The Syndicate, ORE, Thukker Tribe, Sisters of EVE, the Society of Conscious Thought). Militias, pirates, Mordu's Legion, the Triglavian and EDENCOM factions, The Sanctuary and Food Relief can't be converted to.

Pick **CONCORD** as the corporation:
1. A table lists every store your CONCORD LP converts to, grouped by rate: what your CONCORD LP gives there, and the store's **best ISK per CONCORD LP** once it's read. Click a store to open it on its own.
2. **Read every store** reads them all, one after the other (it takes a while the first time).
3. The offer list then holds CONCORD's own offers **and** every read store's, with a **Store** column. **LP** is in CONCORD LP (the store's price ÷ 0.8 or ÷ 0.4, rounded up; hover it for the store's own price), so **ISK per LP** and **You can buy** compare everything in CONCORD LP. An offer that several stores sell at the same rate shows once ("Caldari Navy and 12 more").

The conversion rates aren't given by CCP's data: they come from the players' wiki (EVE University). If the game shows a different rate, the game is right.
