**Market → LP store.**

## Your loyalty points
LP each pilot holds per corporation, most first (needs the **Loyalty points** permission). Click a corporation to open its store.

## LP store
Any NPC corporation's store (the one where you have the most LP by default), every offer in **one list**, best **ISK per LP** first.
1. Pick **Sell items at**: the [pricing](Pricings) you'd sell the reward in.
2. Pick **Buy other items at**: the pricing for the extra items some offers ask for.

Per offer:
- **Sells for:** the item's price × quantity, after your sales tax.
- **Costs:** the offer's ISK plus the other items it asks for.
- **Profit**, **ISK per LP** = (sells for − costs) ÷ LP.
- **You can buy:** how many times your pilots' LP with that corporation pays for it.
- **Sold per day** in that pricing's region: red when under one offer's worth a day (slow to sell).

An item with no price leaves its offer's profit **unknown** (never 0). Blueprint copies usually have no price. Broker fees and Analysis Kredits aren't counted.
