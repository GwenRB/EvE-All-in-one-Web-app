**Pilots → Omega.** ESI doesn't share subscription dates, so **enter when each account's Omega ends**. Pilots with the same account name share one date.

- **Seen at last sync** comes from ESI: a pilot whose skills run below their trained levels is on Alpha.
- Accounts ending within 7 days show on [To-Do](To-Do).
- The **Calendar** shows the end dates week by week (‹ › to move, **Today** to come back).

## Paying for Omega
How you renew, so the [Forecast](Overview) can take it off:
- **Include in forecast**
- **Renew for** (a period, or a number of days)
- **PLEX per renewal** and **ISK per PLEX**

These are the same settings as on the Overview: changing them here changes them there too.
