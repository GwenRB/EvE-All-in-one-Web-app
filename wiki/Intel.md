**Intel**, in the **Other** menu (a red count on the menu and the page when something needs attention). What needs your attention from your pilots' in-game notifications, and where people are dying right now. The full list of notifications is on the [Misc](Misc#in-game-notifications) page.

## Needs attention
From the last 3 days: structures attacked, shields or armour lost (with when the reinforcement ends), low fuel, low power or services offline, wars declared, moon chunks ready (needs the **Notifications** permission). The Intel tab counts them until you click **Mark all as seen**.

## Most violent systems (3 hours) and Low-sec systems (3 hours)
On the right of the page (under it on a narrow screen), like DOTLAN's front page: the 10 null-sec systems and the 10 low-sec systems with the most ships and pods killed in the last 3 hours, with their region. Click a column title to sort.

ESI gives kills one hour at a time, so the tool keeps each hourly reading while it's open and adds up the last three. If it hasn't been open that long, the panel says how many hours it counts. System names come from the map: load it once on the [Map](Map-and-jump-planner) page.
