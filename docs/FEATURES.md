# Features and agreed rules

Tab bar: **Overview · To-Do · Map · Industry ▾ (PI Management, Reactions management, Build planning, Orders management, Jobs) · Planets · Market ▾ (Prices, Trading board, Suppliers, Contract management, Shortages) · Stock ▾ (Assets, Stock management) · Pilots ▾ (Characters, Skills, Omega, Fittings) · Tools ▾ (Misc) · Settings · Notes · Profit and loss**. Menus open on hover or click; the menu holding the open page shows its name. Pages can be hidden in Settings (Settings always stays).

## On every page (user's rules)
- **Tables:** click a column title to sort, again to reverse (remembered, in backups). Before any click a table is A→Z by its first column, except deliberate orders, shown by the arrow on their column: Planets by stop time, Contracts oldest first, Wallets by balance, Omega by end date, Forecast history newest first, Assets and Hangars by value, Arbitrage by profit, Citadels and jump range by distance, Jump fuel cheapest first, Ships by race and size, Pricings as listed. Rows sort inside their group; totals stay last.
- **Grouped tables** fold like Reactions' kinds: click a group's title (▾/▸) to hide its rows ("· n hidden"), remembered. Skill matrix, Key skills, Ships you can build (race › class), PI pick, Trading board and Price comparison (category › group), Orders' materials, Assets items (category › group; "Show more" pages through the open groups), Planets (Stopped / Within 24 h / Later / Not running).
- **Panels** fold from their title (▾/▸), remembered; going to a folded panel from a nav rail opens it.
- **Left nav rail** on Overview, To-Do, Skills, Misc, Settings, Reactions, PI, Build planning and Profit and loss (Skills lists the skill matrix groups).
- **Page notes:** how the page's numbers are worked out, folded at the bottom ("How this page works"), only on pages that have some.
- **Pricings:** Price comparison compares with one of your pricings or another market's orders; Jump fuel cost prices isotopes in a chosen pricing (your typed price wins; used by the map and supply chain too); Doctrine shortages picks the citadel through a citadel pricing (or any other known citadel). The Import calculator and Arbitrage keep their own freight fields (user's choice).

## Overview
- **Overview** (page key `forecast`, hash `#overview`; old `#forecast` links still open it) — the Forecast panel, the Calendar, EvE News, Upcoming fleets and Time zones. **Forecast**: left nav; ISK and Omega lines split; clickable legend hides a line *and* removes it from totals; profit per month after Omega; activities (manual weekly incomes) plus linked ones: "Reactions plan" (`linked: "rx"`) and "PI plan" (`linked: "pi"`) kept up to date from those pages (deleting sets `rxGone`/`piGone`; the pages offer "Add to the Forecast" again). A linked plan with items that have no price shows as "unknown" and is left out of the totals (the Planned profit tile and the Per week line say which plan is left out). Calendar of jobs (manufacturing, reactions, copying, invention, research; legend click hides a type, swatch click changes its colour; month starts drawn bold). **EvE News** (right rail on wide screens): the channels picked in Settings → EvE News channels, newest first, all channels in one list (an article two channels carry shows once; the channel's name shows when more than one is on). Built-in channels: the EVE launcher's news list (`launcher.eveonline.com/resources/eve-online/news_v4/en.json`, on; can't be removed, only unticked; its categories (News, New Eden News, Patch notes, Dev blogs…) can be hidden one by one), Patch notes (`eveonline.com/rss/patch-notes`, off) and EVE forums latest topics (`forums.eveonline.com/latest.rss`, off). Any RSS, Atom or JSON feed can be added by its link (checked by reading it first; named after the feed unless a name is given). Channels can be renamed and moved up or down in Settings; an article two channels carry shows under the higher one. All read through `/proxy`, each channel cached an hour in `eve-industry-news`, Refresh button; links open the article without the launcher's tracking (`utm_*` cut). The launcher's slideshow was removed (user's request). **Upcoming fleets** (blank for now): left rail on wide screens, as wide as the news panel, below the page navigation (its top and height follow the nav's height, `--fcnav-h`). On narrower screens both sit at the bottom of the page and appear in the nav. **Time zones**: EVE time (UTC) first, then your cities (Settings → Time zones: rename, move up/down, remove, add any city by its time zone, back to the default list Paris, Beijing, Sydney, Los Angeles, New York), 24-hour clock, the weekday shown when a city's day isn't EVE's (hover for the date and UTC offset). From 1880 px wide it fills the space left of the page navigation, as tall as the navigation; narrower, it sits at the bottom of the page and appears in the nav.
- **To-Do** — left nav (only the lists with something in them, with their sub-lists); Omega to renew (7 days ahead), ongoing contracts, trips for a freight service, buy for reactions, buy for builds (orders).
- **Map** — 3D/2D map, security/sov colouring, structures with their real icons, PI icon, capital ships (freighters hidden, jump freighters shown) with overview bracket icons, clones, homes, incursions; jump planner (fatigue-optimised waits); **Supply chain** (below).

## Industry
- **Build planning** — full build tree, each job placed in the cheapest manufacturing structure (role bonus + best rig via `rigBonus`), reactions in the refinery, invention for T2/T3 (see below), stock use (assets, ore reprocessing, components owned, stock reserved by ongoing orders), "where to buy" (Jita, local citadel market, suppliers) with copy buttons per source, hauling (JF round trips or freight), timeline with pilots and slots (invention counted when no copies are held), left nav. Sends a build to Orders management (with its route data for the supply chain).
  - **Ore instead of minerals** (setting, off by default): asks where you reprocess (a known refinery, or Tatara / Athanor / NPC station / other structure, Tech I/II reprocessing rig, system for the security bonus, tax %) and who (best pilot per ore, or one pilot). For the minerals still to buy (after your stock and your own ore), one linear program (`lpMin`) finds the cheapest mix of ores and minerals bought as they are, with the same sources and Jita import cost as "where to buy" plus the reprocessing tax (on the outputs' estimated value); compressed ores only by default. Ore is rounded up to whole batches (outputs rounded down like the game); kept only when it costs less than the minerals it replaces. **Several reprocessing places** (user's request): each has its own structure, rig, system, tax and pilot (first one = location `P`, others `P:<id>`); one LP over every ore × place, so each ore goes where it comes out cheapest: yield, tax and, when hauling is counted, an estimate of moving the ore there and its minerals on to the structures (`haulRate`: freight rate, or your cheapest JF round trip ÷ hold); what a seller has is shared between places. **Left-over minerals are reused (user's rule):** each counts at what buying it costs (cheapest source; none without a price), comes off the build cost (`res.oreLeft`) and counts in the ore-vs-minerals check, but the mix is chosen for what the build needs, so ore is never bought for its left-overs. The ore is a material to buy going to its reprocessing place, the minerals leave from there for the structures (hauling and the supply chain see both legs). "Ore to buy" section shows the places, the mix (and where each ore goes), yields, what it gives, left-overs and their worth, and the ISK and m³ saved.
  - **Invention:** all sources read (T3: every relic with its own chance/runs/price; relic choice auto or forced); chance = base × (1 + science/30 + encryption/40) × decryptor; only the runs your copies don't cover are invented (mixed copies: the lower ME is counted); decryptors compared on invention cost + material effect (final job's materials where it would be built) + optional value of build time saved (ISK/hour setting); decryptors with no price aren't offered; attempts on average or enough to be 75/90/95 % sure (binomial); laboratory rigs (time and cost) on the science structure; copy and invention jobs spread over science slots in the timeline.
- **Reactions management** — static data formulas (composites, Molecular-Forged, Isotropic neofullerenes / Neurolink by name; intermediates are planned, not offered); kinds as collapsible groups; column-header sorting; balanced-chain instances (one feeding slot usually feeds 2 composite jobs); rigs like Build planning; selling by contract / immediate / sell order; import and export hauling separately, delivery ⇄ reactor runs priced like Build planning; shopping after assets (scope: every hangar or the build system); step 4 React has a **Build-up phase / Full chain** switch and click counters for jobs started; every job of a reaction runs the same number of runs, rounded up ("4 × 123"). Profit per week → Forecast.
- **PI Management (P4 planner)** — recipes from CCP's planet schematics (built-in table as fallback: P3 = 10 of each P2 → 3 per hour; P4 = 6 of each P3 (+ 40 P1) → 1 per hour); P4s grouped "P1 + two P3" / "three P3"; shared pricings; transport delivery ⇄ planets (JF or freight) and optional Jita import/export legs; shopping for N days of stock after assets; output and per-planet drop-off batches; Jita volume share warning; profit per week → Forecast.
- **Orders management** — orders listed oldest sent first; status planned / in progress (sets `startedAt`) / done; materials, components, ore, costs (missing prices flagged); reopen the exact plan; "Account for ongoing orders" in Build planning reserves their stock.
- **Jobs** — industry jobs per pilot, 30-day planning calendar.

## Planets
Colonies of every linked pilot, ordered by when they stop (extractor end or an input running out).

## Market
- **Prices** — import calculator (delivery vs buying station, hauling), price comparison (citadel vs another market, by item/group/category).
- **Trading board** — market watch tables (history columns fill as you refresh; past history isn't fetched) and **Arbitrage** (citadel sell orders → other market).
- **Suppliers** — supplier stock from a link or paste, priced as listed or a % of a pricing. A Google Sheet is read whole, but only its stock tabs (a header row with an item name column and a quantity column; a "Type ID" column is used as is and the item takes the game's name); notes, raw-data and lookup tabs are left out. Pages that build their list in the browser are read from the data link they use (`SUP_DATA`, e.g. Marketbaiters inventory → its `/api/v1/inventory`); a list only shown to signed-in users has to be pasted.
- **Contract management** — client orders listed oldest first, sends, what's missing; the client box remembers past clients.
- **Shortages** — **Doctrine shortages**: fittings marked Doctrine checked against a chosen citadel's sell orders; empty items (nobody sells), low items (under the fits to keep, set per doctrine), full fits on sale, items to seed with a multibuy copy.

## Stock
- **Assets** — personal and corporation hangars, filters, valuation in a chosen pricing (items with no price shown empty and listed), totals of the filtered items.
- **Stock management** — blank placeholder.

## Pilots
- **Characters** — implants, pilot cards (slots, ranges, manufacturing time, **Reactions level**, reprocessing, orders…), roles.
- **Skills** — skill check per blueprint (who can build/invent), key skills, skill matrix.
- **Omega** — Omega dates.
- **Fittings** — paste EFT or import in-game fittings (ESI); who can fly it; **Doctrine** tick box.

## Tools, Settings
- **Misc** — Blueprint calculator (ME research cost/time, savings in a chosen building place, breakeven), jump freighters and Rorqual, jump fuel cost, mining fleet, ships and structures you can build, Quotation, Wallets (last).
- **Settings** — Pilots and backup (Add pilot, Export/Import backup), page visibility, Pricings, ore reprocessing yield, market taxes (skills/standings), jump drive economizers, EvE News channels (show/hide, rename, order, launcher categories, add a feed by link, remove), Time zones (the Overview's clocks), citadels/structures imports.
- **ESI market reads share one budget** (`esiMarket`): every page's market requests queue together, at most 6 at once, under 90% of ESI's market limit (about 12,000 per 15 minutes per IP; ESI's rate-limit headers correct the count); a refusal pauses them all for as long as ESI asks; the page in view goes first; a small status at the bottom left shows when reads are held back.

## Profit and loss
Page key `pnl` (hash `#profit-and-loss`), a tab of its own at the end of the bar (user's request). Real ISK in and out of every linked wallet, sorted by activity.
- **Data:** ESI wallet journal + market transactions of each pilot (Wallets permission) and the corporation's 7 divisions (Corporation wallets permission, Accountant role), plus industry jobs with `include_completed` (job id → activity). No new permission. Read on every pilot sync (at most every 30 min) and by the page's Refresh. ESI gives 30 days only: every entry is kept in IndexedDB (`pnl`) and in backups; importing a backup **joins** its history with this browser's (`pnlMerge`), never replaces it.
- **No double counting:** purchases and sales come from the transactions (item, quantity, price); the journal's `market_transaction` and `market_escrow` lines are skipped (escrow is a deposit). A pilot's transaction paid by the corporation (`is_personal: false`) is only counted from the corporation's transactions.
- **Between your wallets:** contracts, transfers, donations, courier payments and market trades whose two sides are both yours (linked pilots, corporations whose wallets are read) are left out. Corporation taxes on bounties etc. are not transfers and count.
- **Activities:** Manufacturing, Reactions, Planets (PI), Science, Trading, Hauling, Structures, Personal, Other, plus the user's own (added/removed on the page); "Not assigned" (counted, shown as a tile to sort) and "Left out" (not counted).
- **Sorting rule (first match wins):** the entry's own choice → the item's (purchases, sales, sales tax) → the client's (contracts, transfers, donations, contract deposits) → the kind of entry's → automatic. Automatic: job fees by the job's activity (or the old per-activity ref types), planet taxes/setup → PI, courier contracts and travel fees → Hauling, a contract's fees and deposits follow its price, bounties/missions → Other, clones/skills/insurance → Personal, offices → Structures; items by what they are (`pnlGuess`: PI → Planets; moon materials, intermediates, sold reaction products → Reactions; bought minerals, ore, salvage, components, composites → Manufacturing; sold manufactured items → Manufacturing; datacores, decryptors, sold blueprints → Science; bought ice products → Hauling). Broker fees have no item in ESI unless tied to a transaction: give the kind an activity.
- **Contracts by what they held (user's request):** each contract's items are read once (`/contracts/{id}/items/`, Contracts permission; the issuer's included items, else what was asked) into `pnlData.cont`. A contract with no entry/client/kind choice is sorted item by item (item choice, else `pnlGuess` bought/sold). Its price is shared between the items by their value in the pricing picked on the page (`state.misc.pnl.price`, default Jita split); with several activities every item needs a price there, otherwise the contract stays whole and Not assigned and the page names the unpriced items (never invented). A one-activity contract with unpriced items goes whole to that activity. The contract's fees and deposits are shared like it. A client's explicit choice wins over the contents. A split contract counts as one entry and shows one row per activity in Entries; its items appear in the Items table.
- **Panels:** summary (period: this/last month, 7/30/90 days, this year, everything; tiles Income, Spending, Profit, Not assigned, Left out; by activity › kind; wallets counted; your activities), By month (everything kept), Items (item × activity, choice per item), Who you dealt with (choice per client), Kinds of entry (choice per kind), Entries (filters, sorted and paged in the renderer, choice per entry). Choices live in `state.misc.pnl` (v1: `item`, `party`, `kind`, `line`, `acts`, `off`, `period`).
- **Forecast link (user's request):** the Forecast reads (never writes) the counted entries: an "Actual profit (cumulative, from Profit and loss)" line over the chart's past span (from the first entry kept) and an "Actual profit" tile (per week over the last 30 days kept, next to Planned profit; how much of it is Not assigned). It isn't added to the total value, which already holds that ISK.

## Supply chain (Map page)
- **Ships (user's request: a pilot can use several):** every JF or Rorqual a pilot can fly is its own row: the one flown now (ESI location + ship type scopes; counted once, not again from the assets), each one docked in their assets, and ships you add by hand ("Another ship", removable); a pilot with none seen gets one of the type you pick. Position: ESI/assets, or set by hand (manual wins until a newer ESI or assets read). Range with JDC, hold with skills/expanders. **A pilot flies one ship at a time and their fatigue goes with them.** To take a ship in another system the pilot flies a shuttle (or pod) there through the gates (user's rule: travel time only, 1 min per gate, no cooldown, no fatigue), or jump clones when that gets them there at least an hour sooner (once every 24 h less 1 h per Infomorph Synchronizing level, from the last clone jump ESI reports); the trip shows which, from where, and when the pilot leaves. Settings per ship in `state.misc.sc.ships` (keys `s:<pilot>:<type>:<n>`, `p:<pilot>`, `m:<id>`); older per-pilot settings migrate (`sc.v` 2).
- **Plan ahead** (user's request; `sc.horizon`: 3 days, 1, 2 (default), 4 or 8 weeks): how far the plan reaches. Every reaction cycle and PI refill starting before then is planned, and order moves ready before then (later ones are counted and left for a later plan).
- **Moves:** orders (from `o.route`: materials D→structure, components between structures, product → D; ready/need offsets from the plan's timeline; planned orders have no deadlines, in-progress orders do, from `startedAt`; ESI job end times override readiness), reactions (only if reactor ≠ delivery system: the running cycle's products at its end; then cycle after cycle (Reactions' cycle length): inputs needed when the cycle starts (the next cycle's "as soon as you can": loaded first, never flagged late), products at its end, waiting for that cycle's inputs to land (`into`/`dep` like build products)), PI (see below). Goods made in a structure for a planned order are ready only once the trips bringing that structure's materials have unloaded, plus the build time. Items carry their category so ore (Asteroid category) goes in a Rorqual's ore hold first, then the cargo. Done trips are tracked as fractions per move (`state.misc.sc.done`). **Done from assets (user's request):** when a move is first planned, how much of its items your hangars hold in its destination system is noted (`sc.seen`, with the owners read then); a newer assets read showing more there marks that much of the move done (moves of the same item to the same system share it, the latest noted first). Only the owners read at the time count. The plan lists what was marked this way, with Undo (back as it was, noted again). Goods a job used before the next read can't be seen.
- **PI (user's rule):** the hauler never takes from the planets. The user picks up at the Planets page's harvest time, on the day the week view puts each planet (`pickupAt`, built on `harvestDay`; never in the past; without a harvest time, when the planet stops). The P4 on each planet in the planets' system at that pickup (ESI stock + rate × hours from the last in-game update until it stops or the pickup) is stock arriving in the planets' system then: one move per pickup time to the delivery system. Inputs (when the PI plan has planets) are needed by the first pickup. Further refills until the horizon: each pickup restarts its planets for PI Management's stock days, so the next pickup is the harvest time after that; its P4 is a full refill's output (the planets' rates × stock days) and waits for that refill's inputs, needed at the first pickup of the round before.
- **Rule: a ship flies every leg.** A trip always starts where that ship's last trip ended; when the pickup is elsewhere it flies there empty first (fuel and time counted). Empty legs are their own rows ("Flying empty to pick up trip N") and dashed lines on the map; the smoke test asserts the legs chain.
- **Planning is global (user's rule): the whole plan is optimized, never one trip at a time.** `scSolve`: a plan is, per ship, the lanes it flies in order; simulating it gives each trip's load, times, fuel, lateness and one score. It starts from a trip-by-trip plan (cheapest per m³, or soonest) and tries up to 6,000 changes in ≤ 4 s (move a trip to another ship/place, swap, add or remove a trip on a lane, ore-in-cargo auto/always/never) with late acceptance hill climbing, seeded so the same inputs give the same plan. **Plan for** (`state.misc.sc.focus`, default `cost`): *Lowest fuel cost* = least total fuel (empty legs included), then soonest delivery; *Fastest delivery* = least total delivery time, then fuel. Lateness against need times always weighs most. The panel shows the saving against the trip-by-trip plan.
- **Loading a trip:** most urgent goods first (need time, then ready). Goods that need a cargo hold go in the cargo; ore in the ore hold; ore goes in the cargo only when no other goods on that lane (ready now or later) will need the room — so a Rorqual isn't left running cargo-only trips. A ship waits at a pickup at most "hold goods at most N hours", and only while more goods add to its load. Timing: reposition just in time, load 10 min, gates 1.5 min, jumps 3 min + activation timers/fatigue via `simulateJumps`; cynos assumed everywhere (user's choice); lateness flagged against need times.
- **Trips no ship can do** (too big for every hold, or no route) are kept in `state.misc.sc.freight` until the next plan: a toast when new ones appear, a red count on the To-Do tab, a "Trips for a freight service" panel on To-Do and in the supply chain panel, and "Sent by freight" marks their moves done.
- **User decisions recorded:** positions = ESI + manual override; batching = balance with a max wait; cynos = any of the user's systems; trips can start any time (no play-hour windows).
