# Features and agreed rules

Tab bar: **Overview · To-Do · Map · Industry ▾ (PI Management, Reactions management, Build planning, Orders management, Jobs) · Planets · Market ▾ (Prices, Trading board, Suppliers, Contract management, Contracts browser, Shortages, Profit and loss, LP store) · Stock ▾ (Assets, Stock management) · Pilots ▾ (Characters, Skills, Omega, Fittings) · Other ▾ (Misc, Corporation, Intel, Item database) · Settings · Notes · Tests** (Tests: user's request, 2026-10-28; Intel and the Item database in Other, the Contracts browser in Market: user's request, 2026-10-26; Intel's alert count also shows on the Other menu). Menus open on hover or click; the menu holding the open page shows its name. Pages can be hidden in Settings (Settings always stays).

## On every page (user's rules)
- **Tables:** click a column title to sort, again to reverse (remembered, in backups). Before any click a table is A→Z by its first column, except deliberate orders, shown by the arrow on their column: Planets by stop time, Contracts oldest first, Wallets by balance, Omega by end date, Forecast history newest first, Assets and Hangars by value, Arbitrage by profit, Citadels and jump range by distance, Jump fuel cheapest first, Ships by race and size, Pricings as listed. Rows sort inside their group; totals stay last.
- **Grouped tables** fold like Reactions' kinds: click a group's title (▾/▸) to hide its rows ("· n hidden"), remembered. Skill matrix, Key skills, Ships you can build (race › class), PI pick, Trading board and Price comparison (category › group), Orders' materials, Assets items (category › group; "Show more" pages through the open groups), Planets (Stopped / Within 24 h / Later / Not running).
- **Panels** fold from their title (▾/▸), remembered; going to a folded panel from a nav rail opens it.
- **Filters** (user's request, like jEveAssets): every table that had filters (Assets items, Profit and loss's Entries and Items, Intel, the skill matrix, Ships you can build) and the Contracts browser and Item database have a Filters bar (`cfMount` / `cfApply`, shared): conditions on the table's columns (text: contains / doesn't contain / is / isn't / starts / ends with; numbers: more than, at least, less than, at most, equals, isn't, between; dates: in the last / more than … days ago / in the next N days, on or after, before; lists: is / isn't; every kind: is empty / isn't empty), all or any of them, each can be switched off, sets saved by name per table (`state.misc.cf[key]`, v1: `match`, `rules`, `saved`, `cur`; in backups). They add to the page's own search and selects. An unknown value (no price, no date) never passes a comparison ("is empty" finds it). Amounts read like `parseIsk` (1.5b, 200m). On Assets, conditions test each stack, and like the search they look in every hangar (the Status column narrows to counted / not counted / in a contract).
- **Left nav rail** on Overview, To-Do, Skills, Misc, Settings, Reactions, PI, Build planning, Profit and loss, Corporation and Item database (Skills lists the skill matrix groups).
- **Page notes:** how the page's numbers are worked out, folded at the bottom ("How this page works"), only on pages that have some.
- **Pricings:** Price comparison compares with one of your pricings or another market's orders; Jump fuel cost prices isotopes in a chosen pricing (your typed price wins; used by the map and supply chain too); Doctrine shortages picks the citadel through a citadel pricing (or any other known citadel). The Import calculator and Arbitrage keep their own freight fields (user's choice).

## Header
- **Server status** (public ESI `/status/`, every 2 min): online and players, VIP mode, not answering. **Back online at** (user's request; system indices, bounty payouts etc. follow it): the pill says "Online since HH:MM EVE" from ESI's `start_time` for a day after it; the hover gives it with your local time too. **Downtime**: from 11:00 EVE time until the server says it started again (at most 20 min without a status): the pill says so and syncs, market reads (`esiMarket` answers 503, prices keep what they had), the adjusted prices, sovereignty, incursions and kills wait (`esiDown`).
- **Logins sync in the background** (user's request, 2026-10-27: many characters are added in a row): a login only links the pilot (`linkChar`: refresh token saved, a card with no skills yet, its scopes) and returns; the full read (`applyToken`) is queued (`bgSync`), one pilot at a time (`BG_SYNC_MAX`: two pilots of one corporation would both read its wallets, assets and contracts), a toast when each ends. `syncChar` never reads a pilot twice at once (a second call waits for the running one, `syncRuns`) and takes a pilot out of the queue when synced by hand. The login panel (`#esi-sync-bg`), the Characters table and the pilot cards show Syncing… / Waiting to sync (`syncState`). Closing the page drops the queue: those pilots stay linked, never synced, until Sync now / Sync all.

## Industry
- **Build planning** — **Materials needed** (user's request 2026-10-27, after the time tiles): raw materials (needed, from your stock, to buy; ore bought stands for its minerals, so ore rows aren't listed) and components built on the way (or owned), grouped and folding, with Copy raw materials. **Public contracts** (on demand, a trade hub's region): every public item exchange there (all pages, kept 30 min), the items read only of contracts whose volume is a whole number of products or whose title names it (60 cheapest), and with invention copies of the blueprint by title (40); only contracts holding that item alone and asking nothing count. Says whether buying beats building (per unit, everything the plan counts; unknown when the build has unpriced items) and copies vs inventing per run (`pcCheck`). full build tree, each job placed in the cheapest manufacturing structure (role bonus + best rig via `rigBonus`), reactions in the refinery, invention for T2/T3 (see below), stock use (assets, ore reprocessing, components owned, stock reserved by ongoing orders), "where to buy" (Jita, local citadel market, suppliers) with copy buttons per source, hauling (JF round trips or freight), timeline with pilots and slots (invention counted when no copies are held), left nav. Sends a build to Orders management (with its route data for the supply chain).
  - **Ore instead of minerals** (setting, off by default): asks where you reprocess (a known refinery, or Tatara / Athanor / NPC station / other structure, Tech I/II reprocessing rig, system for the security bonus, tax %) and who (best pilot per ore, or one pilot). For the minerals still to buy (after your stock and your own ore), one linear program (`lpMin`) finds the cheapest mix of ores and minerals bought as they are, with the same sources and Jita import cost as "where to buy" plus the reprocessing tax (on the outputs' estimated value); compressed ores only by default. Ore is rounded up to whole batches (outputs rounded down like the game); kept only when it costs less than the minerals it replaces. **Several reprocessing places** (user's request): each has its own structure, rig, system, tax and pilot (first one = location `P`, others `P:<id>`); one LP over every ore × place, so each ore goes where it comes out cheapest: yield, tax and, when hauling is counted, an estimate of moving the ore there and its minerals on to the structures (`haulRate`: freight rate, or your cheapest JF round trip ÷ hold); what a seller has is shared between places. **Left-over minerals are reused (user's rule):** each counts at what buying it costs (cheapest source; none without a price), comes off the build cost (`res.oreLeft`) and counts in the ore-vs-minerals check, but the mix is chosen for what the build needs, so ore is never bought for its left-overs (user: fine to ignore by-products' worth when choosing). **Ore is hauled compressed (user's rule):** every ore's hauling (the estimate in the choice, the import from Jita and the real hauling) counts the volume of its "Compressed …" form (`oreHaulVol`), so uncompressed ore is assumed compressed before it moves. **Your own ore (user's rule):** reprocessed in the system where its minerals are used, at your best refinery there (`refineryIn`: a known Athanor / Tatara of your corporation or the imported industry structures, with its reprocessing rigs and the system's security; none known there: that ore isn't reprocessed and the plan warns "No refinery detected" for that system) by your best pilot for that ore. Ore already in that system goes first, then ore in the delivery system (hauled there compressed: "Own ore to reprocess" moves); ore elsewhere is pointed out, not used. Its minerals are then used where they're made (no move from the delivery system). The Settings choice "Build planning uses the best yield / the best possible" was removed. The ore is a material to buy going to its reprocessing place, the minerals leave from there for the structures (hauling and the supply chain see both legs). "Ore to buy" section shows the places, the mix (and where each ore goes), yields, what it gives, left-overs and their worth, and the ISK and m³ saved.
  - **Invention:** all sources read (T3: every relic with its own chance/runs/price; relic choice auto or forced); chance = base × (1 + science/30 + encryption/40) × decryptor; only the runs your copies don't cover are invented (mixed copies: the lower ME is counted); decryptors compared on invention cost + material effect (final job's materials where it would be built) + optional value of build time saved (ISK/hour setting); decryptors with no price aren't offered; attempts on average or enough to be 75/90/95 % sure (binomial); laboratory rigs (time and cost) on the science structure; copy and invention jobs spread over science slots in the timeline.

## Profit and loss
Page key `pnl` (hash `#profit-and-loss`), a tab of its own at the end of the bar (user's request). Real ISK in and out of every linked wallet, sorted by activity.
- **Data:** ESI wallet journal + market transactions of each pilot (Wallets permission) and the corporation's 7 divisions (Corporation wallets permission, Accountant role), plus industry jobs with `include_completed` (job id → activity). No new permission. Read on every pilot sync (at most every 30 min) and by the page's Refresh. ESI gives 30 days only: every entry is kept in IndexedDB (`pnl`) and in backups; importing a backup **joins** its history with this browser's (`pnlMerge`), never replaces it.
- **No double counting:** purchases and sales come from the transactions (item, quantity, price); the journal's `market_transaction` and `market_escrow` lines are skipped (escrow is a deposit). A pilot's transaction paid by the corporation (`is_personal: false`) is only counted from the corporation's transactions.
- **Between your wallets:** contracts, transfers, donations, courier payments and market trades whose two sides are both yours (linked pilots, corporations whose wallets are read) are left out. Corporation taxes on bounties etc. are not transfers and count.
- **Activities:** Manufacturing, Reactions, Planets (PI), Science, Trading, Hauling, Structures, Personal, Other, plus the user's own (added/removed on the page); "Not assigned" (counted, shown as a tile to sort) and "Left out" (not counted).
- **Sorting rule (first match wins):** the entry's own choice → the item's (purchases, sales, sales tax) → the client's (contracts, transfers, donations, contract deposits) → the kind of entry's → automatic. Automatic: job fees by the job's activity (or the old per-activity ref types), planet taxes/setup → PI, courier contracts and travel fees → Hauling, a contract's fees and deposits follow its price, bounties/missions → Other, clones/skills/insurance → Personal, offices → Structures; items by what they are (`pnlGuess`: PI → Planets; moon materials, intermediates, sold reaction products → Reactions; bought minerals, ore, salvage, components, composites → Manufacturing; sold manufactured items → Manufacturing; datacores, decryptors, sold blueprints → Science; bought ice products → Hauling). Broker fees have no item in ESI unless tied to a transaction: give the kind an activity.
- **Contracts by what they held (user's request):** each contract's items are read once (`/contracts/{id}/items/`, Contracts permission; the issuer's included items, else what was asked) into `pnlData.cont`. ## Supply chain (Map page)
- **Danger:** a leg that goes by gate (its gate stretches, or all of it when it has no jump route) through a dangerous system, or **jumps into** one (user's request: the cyno there can be caught), shows ⚠ "jumps to …" / "gates through …" with the systems and kills, and a notice counts those trips. Routes aren't changed: it's a warning (`legDanger`).
- **Ships (user's request: a pilot can use several):** every JF or Rorqual a pilot can fly is its own row: the one flown now (ESI location + ship type scopes; counted once, not again from the assets), each one docked in their assets, and ships you add by hand ("Another ship", removable); a pilot with none seen gets one of the type you pick. Position: ESI/assets, or set by hand (manual wins until a newer ESI or assets read). Range with JDC, hold with skills/expanders. **A pilot flies one ship at a time and their fatigue goes with them.** To take a ship in another system the pilot flies a shuttle (or pod) there through the gates (user's rule: travel time only, 1 min per gate, no cooldown, no fatigue), or jump clones when that gets them there at least an hour sooner (once every 24 h less 1 h per Infomorph Synchronizing level, from the last clone jump ESI reports); the trip shows which, from where, and when the pilot leaves. Settings per ship in `state.misc.sc.ships` (keys `s:<pilot>:<type>:<n>`, `p:<pilot>`, `m:<id>`); older per-pilot settings migrate (`sc.v` 2).
- **Plan ahead** (user's request; `sc.horizon`: 3 days, 1, 2 (default), 4 or 8 weeks): how far the plan reaches. Every reaction cycle and PI refill starting before then is planned, and order moves ready before then (later ones are counted and left for a later plan).
- **Moves:** orders (from `o.route`: materials D→structure, components between structures, product → D; ready/need offsets from the plan's timeline; planned orders have no deadlines, in-progress orders do, from `startedAt`; ESI job end times override readiness), reactions (only if reactor ≠ delivery system: the running cycle's products at its end; then cycle after cycle (Reactions' cycle length): inputs needed when the cycle starts (the next cycle's "as soon as you can": loaded first, never flagged late), products at its end, waiting for that cycle's inputs to land (`into`/`dep` like build products)), PI (see below). Goods made in a structure for a planned order are ready only once the trips bringing that structure's materials have unloaded, plus the build time. Items carry their category so ore (Asteroid category) goes in a Rorqual's ore hold first, then the cargo. Done trips are tracked as fractions per move (`state.misc.sc.done`). **Done from assets (user's request):** when a move is first planned, how much of its items your hangars hold in its destination system is noted (`sc.seen`, with the owners read then); a newer assets read showing more there marks that much of the move done (moves of the same item to the same system share it, the latest noted first). Only the owners read at the time count. The plan lists what was marked this way, with Undo (back as it was, noted again). Goods a job used before the next read can't be seen.
- **PI (user's rule):** the hauler never takes from the planets. The user picks up at the Planets page's harvest time, on the day the week view puts each planet (`pickupAt`, built on `harvestDay`; never in the past; without a harvest time, when the planet stops). The P4 on each planet in the planets' system at that pickup (ESI stock + rate × hours from the last in-game update until it stops or the pickup) is stock arriving in the planets' system then: one move per pickup time to the delivery system. Inputs (when the PI plan has planets) are needed by the first pickup. Further refills until the horizon: each pickup restarts its planets for PI Management's stock days, so the next pickup is the harvest time after that; its P4 is a full refill's output (the planets' rates × stock days) and waits for that refill's inputs, needed at the first pickup of the round before.
- **Rule: a ship flies every leg.** A trip always starts where that ship's last trip ended; when the pickup is elsewhere it flies there empty first (fuel and time counted). Empty legs are their own rows ("Flying empty to pick up trip N") and dashed lines on the map; the smoke test asserts the legs chain.
- **Planning is global (user's rule): the whole plan is optimized, never one trip at a time.** `scSolve`: a plan is, per ship, the lanes it flies in order; simulating it gives each trip's load, times, fuel, lateness and one score. It starts from a trip-by-trip plan (cheapest per m³, or soonest) and tries up to 6,000 changes in ≤ 4 s (move a trip to another ship/place, swap, add or remove a trip on a lane, ore-in-cargo auto/always/never) with late acceptance hill climbing, seeded so the same inputs give the same plan. **Plan for** (`state.misc.sc.focus`, default `cost`): *Lowest fuel cost* = least total fuel (empty legs included), then soonest delivery; *Fastest delivery* = least total delivery time, then fuel. Lateness against need times always weighs most. The panel shows the saving against the trip-by-trip plan.
- **Loading a trip:** most urgent goods first (need time, then ready). Goods that need a cargo hold go in the cargo; ore in the ore hold; ore goes in the cargo only when no other goods on that lane (ready now or later) will need the room — so a Rorqual isn't left running cargo-only trips. A ship waits at a pickup at most "hold goods at most N hours", and only while more goods add to its load. Timing: reposition just in time, load 10 min, gates 1.5 min, jumps 3 min + activation timers/fatigue via `simulateJumps`; cynos assumed everywhere (user's choice); lateness flagged against need times.
- **Trips no ship can do** (too big for every hold, or no route) are kept in `state.misc.sc.freight` until the next plan: a toast when new ones appear, a red count on the To-Do tab (with skill queues to fill, `todoBadge`), a "Trips for a freight service" panel on To-Do and in the supply chain panel, and "Sent by freight" marks their moves done.
- **User decisions recorded:** positions = ESI + manual override; batching = balance with a max wait; cynos = any of the user's systems; trips can start any time (no play-hour windows).
